# arc42 Notes

1. **Introduction and Goals:** Describes why the system exists, its essential requirements, the top quality goals and the stakeholders with their expectations.
2. **Architecture Constraints:** Lists the technical, organizational and convention constraints the architecture must respect and cannot freely change.
3. **System Scope and Context:** Defines the system boundary and shows its users and external systems together with the interfaces between them.
4. **Solution Strategy:** Summarizes the fundamental decisions and approaches, such as technology choices and top-level patterns, that shape the architecture.
5. **Building Block View:** Shows the static decomposition of the system into containers and components and how they relate to each other.
6. **Runtime View:** Describes how the building blocks interact at runtime in important scenarios, usually as sequence diagrams.
7. **Deployment View:** Describes the technical infrastructure and environments the system runs on and how the building blocks are mapped to them.
8. **Cross-cutting Concepts:** Documents patterns and solutions that apply across many parts of the system, such as error handling, logging, security and validation.
9. **Architecture Decisions:** Records important, expensive-to-change decisions as ADRs, including their context, rationale and consequences.
10. **Quality Requirements:** Makes quality goals concrete and measurable through a quality tree and testable quality scenarios.
11. **Risks and Technical Debts:** Lists known risks and technical debts, ordered by priority, together with mitigation measures.
12. **Glossary:** Defines the important domain and technical terms so that all stakeholders share the same vocabulary.
